These are the app's own files. None of them come from another program.

keylight-mark.svg
    The mark in the top bar: an orange bolt on a peach chip. Drawn for
    this app.

ak820-caps.json
    Where the eighty-two keys sit on the AK820, and what is written on
    them, as numbers. The board in the app is drawn from this file, so
    the keycaps are in the right places and the colour on a key is the
    colour of that key. The numbers were measured once from a picture of
    the board; the places the keys sit are facts about the keyboard.

There is no picture of the board here any more. The app draws it.
