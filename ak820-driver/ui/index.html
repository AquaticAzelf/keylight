How this app works

One file, no installer, offline

Keylight is one file you can copy to another computer. It needs no installer, writes nothing to the registry, and never reaches the internet. Copy the folder to move your profiles and macros with you.

Keylight is a third-party tool. It is not made by, or endorsed by, AJAZZ. “AK820” is used only to say which keyboard it works with. The keyboard is made by 深圳市黑爵同创电子科技有限公司.

MIT licence. The keyboard’s protocol was worked out from the EVision V2 driver in OpenRGB, and confirmed against the board.

How this works

Please read before you turn it on

These macros run on the computer, not inside the keyboard. The board itself keeps no macros, and nothing about it changes. While the app is recording, the keys you press are held back so they do not do anything else.

The window is too small

Make it at least 620 pixels wide and the controls will fit. The app needs about 1100 by 720 to show everything at once.
